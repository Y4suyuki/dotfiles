# Playbook: P3 - Thought Piece & Engineering Culture

Used for tech lead reflections, organizational design, development philosophy, and career insights.

## Writing Rules
- **Ground in Tangible Incidents:** Never write pure abstract theory. Anchor points in actual friction encountered during projects, code reviews, or team handoffs.
- **Avoid Platitudes:** Ban generic advice like "communication is key," "alignment matters," or "psychological safety is important" unless accompanied by the specific mechanism or protocol used to achieve it.
- **Honest Dilemmas:** Acknowledge the downsides of your own approach; avoid presenting solutions as silver bullets. Show vulnerability in technical or leadership decisions.

## Standard Section Pattern
1. **The Core Observation:** A counter-intuitive realization, recurring dilemma, or anti-pattern observed in the wild.
2. **The Friction:** Why conventional best practices or naive approaches fell short in this specific situation.
3. **The Mechanism:** What concrete process, mental model, tool, or boundary was introduced to resolve the friction.
4. **The Honest Assessment:** What it cost to implement, what it gained, and where this mental model does not apply.
